Gold Coast, Australia
Business Profiles

The Gold Coast is one of Australia’s most established marine industry centres, home to more than 500 marine businesses spanning advanced manufacturing, refit and repair, marine technology, design, professional services, and superyacht support. This mature and highly connected ecosystem has earned a global reputation for innovation, capability, and quality.

DockPro

DockPro’s SeaPen® is an Australian-made, water-level dry-docking system designed to make launching, docking and maintaining boats easier. Suitable for most recreational and light commercial vessels approximately 6 to 20 metres long, SeaPen accommodates monohulls, catamarans, tri-hulls, pontoon boats, wake boats and trailer-sailers with outboards, stern drives, jet drives or V-drives.

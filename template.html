Business Profiles

The Gold Coast is one of Australia’s most established marine industry centres, home to more than 500 marine businesses spanning advanced manufacturing, refit and repair, marine technology, design, professional services, and superyacht support. This mature and highly connected ecosystem has earned a global reputation for innovation, capability, and quality.
